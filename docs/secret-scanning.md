# Runtime environment/configuration secret coverage (2026-10-22)

Scope: exact installed check/engine mappings and collector routing in this checkout. Module docstrings were not taken as proof. `content_secret_scans=true` plus an explicitly named check is required for every offline literal scan below; category/framework/all-check selection may show an UNKNOWN check, but does not authorize content collection. The pinned Kingfisher 1.4.0 and TruffleHog 3.92.5 adapters scan offline without credential verification. No default automatic TH/KF sweep of all environment variables exists.

`engine.py` maps named IDs to `_content_secret_scan_kinds`; the collectors require that internal grant as well as the boolean. The check catalog installs the cited predicates. There are automatic metadata/name heuristics outside this table; they do not constitute literal TH/KF scanning and do not establish absence of secrets. Broad unselected workload env, dynamic runtime overrides, secret stores, masked native values, and unsupported source types remain unsupported for literal scanning.


Enable content scanning and select the matching checks and targets before launching. A completed scan covers its requested selections; it does not imply that every optional source was inspected. Denied, truncated or unsupported reads cannot establish absence of secrets.
