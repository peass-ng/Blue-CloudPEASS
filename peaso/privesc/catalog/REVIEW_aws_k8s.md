# Accuracy review: AWS and Kubernetes privesc catalog

Scope: `catalog/aws/*.yaml` (154 entries at start, 155 after review) and `catalog/k8s/*.yaml` (61 entries), plus `aws/_excluded_a.yaml`, `aws/_excluded_b.yaml` and `k8s/_excluded.yaml`.
Sources of truth: hacktricks-cloud HEAD `a53b46392c` (the revision the entries already cite; the brief mentions `6bdd8e89e6`, but `a53b46392c` is the actual checkout HEAD), plus official AWS and Kubernetes docs.

Result: 215 existing entries reviewed. 15 existing entries changed (the 14 non-NEW rows in the table, plus a cross-reference note on aws-sagemaker-updatedomain-createuserprofile-passrole) and 1 entry added. 12 issues flagged without a fix (one, aws-ec2-launchtemplate-version-asg, was reviewed and found correct).
Follow-up round: 10 of the 12 flagged issues are resolved (see "Follow-up changes"). Of the other 2, aws-ec2-launchtemplate-version-asg was found correct, and the deduplication of aws-organizations-management-to-member-accounts is left to the evaluator. 9 existing entries were changed, 2 duplicate entries were removed and 2 entries were added. The AWS catalogue now has 154 valid entries plus the StackSet entry, which is pending.
Validator after the follow-up (`scripts/peaso_validate_privesc_catalog.py --permissions`): 465 valid entries (aws 154, azure 128, gcp 122, k8s 61), 1 error and 0 warnings.
- The error is `unknown selector key 'assumable_by_pivot_role'` on aws-cloudformation-passrole-stackset. It is expected: the key is listed in SCHEMA_EXTENSIONS.md under "REVIEW FOLLOW-UP", and the error clears once the evaluator registers the key. Until then the entry is dropped.
- No duplicate ids. Non-strict `load_catalog(hacktricks_root=...)` gives the same single error and 0 anchor warnings, and strict mode raises on it.
- `tests/test_peaso_privesc.py`: 25 passed.

## Excluded-file review

- `aws/_excluded_a.yaml` and `aws/_excluded_b.yaml`: no wrongly excluded escalations found.
  - The Lambda function-URL exclusion is correct. Since October 2025, new function URLs require both lambda:InvokeFunctionUrl and lambda:InvokeFunction (https://docs.aws.amazon.com/lambda/latest/dg/urls-auth.html), so that path is subsumed by the createfunction + invokefunction entry.
  - The ECS UpdateService desired-count, SNS/SQS/EventBridge fixed-code invocation and KMS/Secrets Manager data-access exclusions are justified.
- `k8s/_excluded.yaml`: all exclusions are justified (network, data, policy-bypass or duplicate). Heading-coverage check: every technique heading in the K8s and OpenShift HackTricks pages maps to an entry or an exclusion.

## Systemic issues

1. `{target.arn}` on entity targets (`aws_account_admin`) makes edges always unknown. This is fixed for the group self-escalations and, in the follow-up, for aws-iam-modify-assumable-role-policy (`{source.assumable_role_arn}`).
2. The command regex has false positives: any "AWS <word> <word>" phrase in text matches. Text was reworded in 4 entries. Consider tightening `_COMMANDS` to exclude capitalised "AWS".
3. Service-specific ECR consumer entries duplicated aws-ecr-push-consumed-tag. They were removed and merged in the follow-up.
4. The `ecr_tag_immutability` blocker was missing on the private ECR entries. Note that the evaluator does not yet act on this blocker.
5. Some requirements were too narrow (sts:AssumeRole in CreateRole) and some cheaper variants were missing (SageMaker CreateUserProfile added; Batch RegisterJobDefinition and SSM CreateDocument were added in the follow-up, anchored to the closest HackTricks sections).
6. HackTricks can be wrong about permissions (Glue UpdateJob without PassRole). When the HackTricks text and the Service Authorization Reference dependent actions disagree, the catalog now follows the Service Authorization Reference.
